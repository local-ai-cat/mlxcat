    Three things this board does not say

    Batched gemma is not token-exact. The gemma concurrency win visible below rests on a gate that loads gemma through LLMModelFactory, while production loads it through VLMModelFactory — different attention implementations. On the production path, gemma-4-12B diverges from a serial run in 3 of 4 rows at width 4, with a clean width-1 control. Tracked as KNOWN-FAILURES §1f; nothing has been reverted, and the trade is an open decision.

    Peak is per process, not per cell. One server process serves every tier of a model and MLX does not return its buffer free list on its own, so a cell measured after a 16k tier inherits that tier's high-water mark. This is why a new tier can look like a regression on cells that did not regress.

    Competitor rows are older than ours. They were measured 2026-08-21 to 08-22 and have not moved since. Every engine here improves over time; a competitor line going stale flatters us, and the honest fix is to re-measure them, not to widen the gap on paper.

    The machine drifts between passes. In pass 5 an unchanged gpt-oss configuration measured 9.8% slower than in pass 4 — same code, same flags, same host. Our line moves with machine state; the frozen competitor lines do not. Treat a single-digit-percent move between passes as weather, and read the direction of a series rather than the gap in any one cell.
